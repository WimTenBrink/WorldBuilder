# Aerthos

**Style:** Fantasy

## Core Information

- **Technology Level:** Bronze Age / early Iron Age, enhanced by magic
- **Calendar:** The Calendar of Aerthos - The Calendar of Aerthos is based on the orbits of the two moons, Lunara and Nyx, and the solar cycle. A year consists of 13 cycles of Lunara, with each cycle lasting 28 days, totaling 364 days. An additional 'Day of Balance' is added every four years to align with the solar year. Months are named after prominent constellations and significant celestial events, reflecting both Elven and Human traditions.

## Celestial Objects

### Stars
- **Solus:** The life-giving sun of Aerthos, around which all planets and moons orbit. It is the primary source of light and warmth, revered by all races, though often seen differently: as a symbol of enlightenment by Elves, of strength by Humans, and of raw power by Orcs.

### Planets
- **Ignis:** The closest planet to Solus, fiery and tempestuous. Believed to be the domain of elementals and raw magical energy, it appears as a bright, pulsating star in the dawn sky.
- **Viridia:** A lush, green world, visible as a morning star. It is associated with fertility, growth, and the natural cycle of life. Human druids often draw power from its alignment.
- **Celeris:** A swift, nimble planet often seen darting across the sky. Believed to influence trade, communication, and swift journeys. Merchants and messengers often pray to its guiding light.
- **Justitia:** A large, golden-hued gas giant, named after the patron deity of justice and prosperity. Its slow, majestic orbit is seen as a symbol of stability and order.
- **Bellum:** A red, turbulent world, associated with conflict, courage, and the forge. Orcish shamans claim to hear the drums of their ancestors from its distant light.
- **Glacius:** A shimmering, ice-blue world, distant and mysterious. Elves believe it is a realm of ancient spirits and forgotten lore, its cold light holding secrets of the cosmos.
- **Umbra:** The outermost known planet, cloaked in perpetual shadow. It is rarely seen and shrouded in superstition, often associated with the unknown, death, and primordial chaos.

### Moons
- **Lunara, the Silver Weaver:** The larger and brighter of Aerthos's two moons, Lunara is associated with tides, magic, and life. Its silvery light is revered by the Elves, who believe it amplifies their arcane abilities. Lunara governs the cycles of the Elven calendar.
- **Nyx, the Crimson Hunter:** Smaller and darker, Nyx often appears as a reddish orb in the night sky. It is associated with shadows, hidden knowledge, and the wilder aspects of magic. Orcs often associate Nyx with battle fury and primal instincts, believing it calls them to war.

### Constellations
- **The Spear of Arion:** A prominent constellation resembling a warrior with a spear, said to represent the first human king, 'King Arion'. It is used by human navigators and celebrated in their winter festivals.
- **The Serpent of the Deep:** A delicate, flowing constellation resembling a mythical sea serpent. Elves believe it guides their ships and protects them from storms. It features heavily in Elven maritime legends.
- **Grom's Tusk:** A fierce cluster of stars forming the head of a charging boar. Orcish shamans interpret its movements for omens of war and plentiful hunts, often appearing during the harsh winter months.

## Geography

### Oceans
- **The Mare Aetherium:** The vast, deep ocean that surrounds the known continents of Aerthos. It is the primary domain of Elven exploration and trade, its currents carrying ancient magic. Many mythical creatures are said to dwell in its uncharted depths.

### Seas
- **The Azure Straits:** A large, relatively calm sea bordered by the mainland human kingdoms to the north and the Elven islands to the south. It is the primary trade route between humans and elves, traversed by Elven merchant ships.
- **The Iron Sea:** A rougher, often storm-tossed sea to the west of the human mainland, prone to strong currents and dangerous reefs. It is less traveled by Elven ships but is where the Kingdom of Ironpeak has its few ports.

### Rivers
- **The River Veritas:** The longest river in the Human lands, flowing from the Spine of Aerthos through the Kingdom of Veritas and eventually emptying into the Iron Sea. It is a vital artery for trade and agriculture.
- **The Silverflow:** A swift-flowing river originating in the Verdant Veil and winding through the Kingdom of Eldoria before reaching the coast. Its waters are considered sacred by the priests of Eldoria, believed to possess minor healing properties.

### Major Landmarks
- **The Spine of Aerthos (Mountain Range)** at Southern border of Human Kingdoms (Ironpeak, Veritas): A colossal mountain range forming the southern border of the human kingdoms, riddled with rich veins of copper and tin. Its jagged peaks are home to fierce beasts and ancient dwarven ruins.
- **The Verdant Veil (Forest)** at Central Mainland, between Veritas and Eldoria: A vast, ancient forest on the mainland, known for its towering, moss-draped trees and hidden magical springs. It is a place of mystery and ancient fey encounters, often avoided by common folk.
- **The Sunken Spires of Eldrin (Underwater Ruins)** at Beneath the Azure Straits, near Silverhaven: A series of sunken, crystalline spires that once formed an ancient Elven city before being submerged. Legends say powerful artifacts and forgotten knowledge lie within its watery depths, accessible only to the most skilled Elven divers.
- **The Bastion of Iron (Fortress)** at Coastal cliffs of Kingdom of Ironpeak, overlooking the Iron Sea: A formidable fortress built into the cliffs overlooking the Iron Sea. It serves as the primary defense against Orcish incursions and a key naval base for the Kingdom of Ironpeak.
- **The Scorched Wastes (Volcanic Plateau)** at South of Human Kingdoms, across the Ashfang Gap: A desolate, volcanic plateau to the south, characterized by black rock formations and active geysers. This is the primary territory of the Ashfang Tribes, a harsh land that forges their fierce nature.
