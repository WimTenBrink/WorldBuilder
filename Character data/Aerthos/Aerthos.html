Aerthos

Style: Fantasy

Core Information

Technology Level: Bronze Age / early Iron Age, enhanced by magic

Calendar: The Calendar of Aerthos - The Calendar of Aerthos is based on the orbits of the two moons, Lunara and Nyx, and the solar cycle. A year consists of 13 cycles of Lunara, with each cycle lasting 28 days, totaling 364 days. An additional 'Day of Balance' is added every four years to align with the solar year. Months are named after prominent constellations and significant celestial events, reflecting both Elven and Human traditions.

Countries

The Elven Kingdom of Aethelgard - Monarchy, led by an immortal Arch-King/Queen

Kingdom of Veritas - Monarchy with a Council of Nobles

Kingdom of Ironpeak - Militaristic Monarchy

Kingdom of Eldoria - Theocratic Monarchy, ruled by a Divine Emperor/Empress

The Ashfang Tribes - Nomadic Tribal Confederacy, led by a War-Chief
